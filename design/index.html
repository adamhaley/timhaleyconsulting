Tim Haley Consulting style guide

The design tokens and components for the new site. Everything here comes from styles.css; page comps in this folder use only that stylesheet.

Colour

--color-ink
--color-muted
--color-paper
--color-surface
--color-accent
--color-accent-ink
--color-accent-hover
--color-brand
--color-brand-soft
--color-brand-ink
--color-highlight

The palette pairs deep steel navy with warm off-white paper to feel steady and technical but approachable, and a copper highlight hints at heat treatment. Approved text pairs, all WCAG AA or better: --color-ink and --color-muted on --color-paper or --color-surface; --color-accent (links) on paper or surface; --color-accent-ink on --color-accent or --color-accent-hover; and --color-brand-ink on --color-brand or the brand gradient (and the reverse, for the hero button). Keep --color-highlight for rules, borders, hover marks and focus outlines only, never for text: it meets 3:1 on paper, surface and brand, not 4.5:1.
