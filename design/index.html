The palette pairs deep steel navy with warm off-white paper to feel steady and technical but approachable, and a copper highlight hints at heat treatment. Approved text pairs, all WCAG AA or better: --color-ink (14.8:1 and 13.3:1) and --color-muted (6.8:1 and 6.1:1) on --color-paper or --color-surface; --color-accent (links, 8.6:1 and 7.8:1) on paper or surface; --color-accent-ink on --color-accent (9.2:1) or --color-accent-hover (12.4:1); and --color-brand-ink on --color-brand (14.3:1), --color-brand-soft (11.6:1) or anywhere on the hero band, gradient and copper glow included (8:1 at its warmest). The hero button reverses the pair: --color-brand on brand-ink (14.3:1), or on surface when hovered (13.4:1). Keep --color-rule for hairlines and --color-highlight for rules, borders, hover marks and focus outlines only, never for text: the copper meets 3:1 on paper, surface, brand and brand-soft, not 4.5:1. On the hero, focus outlines switch to brand-ink because the glow lowers the copper's contrast.

Typography

Headings are set in Source Serif 4 (semibold 600, bold 700 for page titles), a sturdy, warm serif that reads as considered and authoritative. Body text and interface labels are set in IBM Plex Sans (regular 400, medium 500, semibold 600), a plain-spoken sans with an engineered feel. Both load from Google Fonts and fall back to Georgia and the system sans. Headings use a tight 1.15 line height and slightly negative letter spacing (−0.015em, −0.025em for display sizes); body text uses a 1.65 line height at a 66-character measure.

Spacing

A 4px base. Small steps space text and controls; large steps separate blocks. Major sections use --section-space, which grows from 48px on phones to 96px on wide screens.

Graphical treatment

Quiet and precise: soft 8px corners, hairline rules in a warm grey, navy-tinted shadows that lift cards only slightly, and one copper accent per element (a top rule, an underline or a focus ring). The brand band carries the only decoration: a low copper glow in its top corner with faint concentric rings, like isotherms radiating from a heat source.
